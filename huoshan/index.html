01 — The Origin

The essence of a dream retreat

The Essence of a Dream

Dream Island · Huoshan was founded in 2020, above the eastern shore of Erhai Lake. Its name comes from founder Ba Xun's lifelong dream: to return to oneself.

Here, architecture does not rise above the landscape. It settles into trees and stone, weaving Bai villages, quiet mountain paths and clear lake light into a slower, more truthful way of living.

02 — Rooted

Rooted in
Bai heritage

Of This Land

Huoshan rests above Shuanglang, a mountain shaped by village life and open horizons. Dream Island responds with rammed earth, rough stone and timber, allowing traditional craft to grow into contemporary living.

03 — Architecture & Art

Huoshan Art Museum

A Landscape Theatre

Designed by architect and artist Ba Xun, the museum is a concrete landscape theatre. Spiralling ramps, geometric light and monumental volumes bring the walker into conversation with mountain, sky and self.

Founder · Architect & Artist

Ba Xun

BA XUN

Dream Island grew from Ba Xun's return to the land and his long journey through it. Drawing on vernacular craft, he distilled his understanding of nature, dwelling and art into a dream you can inhabit—architecture as a living work.

04 — Stays

Seven Collections

Twenty-one rooms and suites are scattered across the forest at different elevations. Rammed earth, rough stone and Canadian maple frame the mountain and lake from every window.
