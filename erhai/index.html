THE HOUSE

On Dajianpang Peninsula, the lake is not a distant view. It is the edge of the house.

Dream Island · Erhai sits within Shuanglang, where lanes, stone walls and water meet. With only seven rooms, the stay feels closer to a private lakeside house than a conventional hotel: intimate enough to notice the changing light, slow enough to let the day remain open.

Individually composed

Rooms are designed one by one rather than repeated from a template, allowing windows, proportions and materials to respond to their position in the house.

02

Natural to the touch

Local stone, original timber, cotton and linen form a quiet material palette, joined by wool rugs and Egyptian-cotton bedding.

03

Small comforts, considered

Private baths, air conditioning, Marshall sound, coffee and tea, a humidifier and Appelles amenities support the stay without taking over it.
