THE PRINCIPLE

D

Dali is often treated as a bridge between bigger names on a map. We believe it works better as a pause: enough time for the lake light to change, for the mountain to set the pace, and for a journey to feel inhabited rather than completed.

At Dream Island, that means allowing three or four nights rather than arriving late and leaving early. Huoshan sits above Shuanglang, away from the old-town circuit. The reward is space; the planning consequence is that onward travel should begin deliberately.

Choose the next place for contrast, not accumulation. Lijiang adds old lanes and higher mountain landscapes. Shangri-La brings altitude and Tibetan cultural context. Chengdu changes the rhythm again through food, neighbourhood life and Sichuan’s wider landscapes.

“The best route is not the one that includes the most places. It is the one that leaves enough room to remember them.”

02

THREE WAYS FORWARD

Begin with pace,
then choose distance.

These are planning shapes, not fixed packages. Season, altitude, interests and the rest of your China journey should decide the final route.

A
THE GENTLE EXTENSION

Dali & Lijiang

For travellers who want a clear change of scene without turning the week into a chain of departures. Stay slowly at Huoshan, then continue north for Lijiang’s courtyards, markets and mountain horizon.

  • Best for a shorter first journey
  • One meaningful onward move
  • Culture, landscape and unhurried stays
B
THE HIGHER LANDSCAPE

Dali, Lijiang & Shangri-La

For guests with more time and comfort at altitude. Lijiang becomes a transition rather than a finish, leading onward to the plateau, Tibetan cultural landscapes and a noticeably different climate.

  • Best with a measured ascent
  • Allow weather and altitude flexibility
  • Strong landscape and cultural contrast
C
THE WIDER SOUTHWEST

Dali, Lijiang & Chengdu

For a fuller Southwest China story. The journey moves between Yunnan’s lake-and-mountain rhythm and Chengdu’s food, neighbourhoods and access to the wider landscapes of Sichuan.

  • Best within a 12–16 day China journey
  • A broader mix of city and nature
  • Private transfers, rail and flights shaped as one route
